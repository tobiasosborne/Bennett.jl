## Session log — 2026-10-04 — differential narrowing landed (2cd65f2): the review witness is refused on the default path; 5y48 closed, sl4h stays open

**What it does** (`src/narrow_differential.jl`, new: `_narrow_candidates`,
`_narrow_differential_compile`, a 64-lane bit-sliced comparator, `_narrow_verdict_cache`;
`_narrow_hybrid` removed from `src/extract/callees.jl`; the Tuple overload in `src/Bennett.jl`).
With `bit_width=W ≠ S` and `optimize=true`, BOTH readings are narrowed — U from the unoptimised IR,
O from the optimised IR.
- Both accepted → both are lowered and compared on raw W-bit output patterns: on every input when
  the narrowed inputs total ≤ 16 bits, on a fixed sample of 4096 above that (corners, single-bit
  patterns, per-argument extremes, seeded splitmix64). An input on which one circuit fails (loop
  guard, dirty ancilla, changed input) and the other does not is a disagreement. Disagreement →
  `ArgumentError` ("refusing to narrow", naming 5y48 / sl4h / mrhg, first differing input with
  both outputs). Agreement → U's circuit.
- Exactly one accepted → used unchecked, as before (documented limitation). Both refused → one
  error carrying both messages. Lowering O refused → U is used; a refusal lowering U propagates.
- `optimize=false` and W == S behave as before.
