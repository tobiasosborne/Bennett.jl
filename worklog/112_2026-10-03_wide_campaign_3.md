**Bennett-6p8j — narrowing comparison constants read at the wrong width (review-1 R4)** (f54feda).
`_narrow_cmp_consts` (src/narrow.jl) allowed 0..2^W-1 for eq/ne and unsigned predicates, but LLVM
hands constants sign-extended (`x == Int8(-1)` and `x == 0xff` are the same `icmp eq x, -1`), so that
was refused even at W=8. New rule, cs = signed i$w value of the constant: (1) a compare left at its
width (W==S, or an i1 compare) accepts any i$w constant; (2) signed orderings need cs to fit
min(W,w) signed bits, constant rewritten to cs; (3) unsigned orderings accept 0..2^(min(W,w)-1)-1,
because folded signed range checks only agree with the source below 2^(W-1); (4) eq/ne is congruence
mod 2^W: cs must be the sign- or zero-extension of its low W bits (cs >= 0 when W>w), excluding the
type maxima -1 and smax(w); a constant that is not an i$w value is refused. The OLD rule accepted
wrong answers under optimize=true (Int8 source): `0<=x<10` -> `icmp ult x, 10`, wrong at W=4 (x=-8,
-7); `x<0||x>12` -> `icmp ugt x, 12`, wrong at W=4 (x=-8..-4); `x >= Int8(127)` -> `icmp eq x, 127`,
true at -1 at W=7 (UInt8 `x >= 0xff`/`x > 0xfe` fold to `x == -1`, `x < 0xff` to `x != -1`). The
reviewer's literal suggestion (accept -1 for eq at W<S) would open exactly that `x >= 0xff` fold, so
`x == Int8(-1)` is accepted only at W=8 and stays refused (pinned) at W<8. New
`test/test_6p8j_narrow_cmp_consts.jl`: Int8/UInt8 x 6 operators (all 10 predicates) x boundary
