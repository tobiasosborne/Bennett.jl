**Bennett-oai0 (test-only, Sonnet).** `mrhg_w_mem` at `bit_width=4, optimize=true` is now a
definite gq1z refusal pin; the dead compile branch and the wrong comment from 6atf are gone. New
fixtures `mrhg_w_localref` (`r = Ref{Int8}(x); r[] += 2; r[] += 3; r[]`) and `mrhg_w_reftup`

both compile at `bit_width=4, optimize=true`, right on all 16 patterns. At `optimize=false`

tree: 212,878 / 212,878 (was 212,834); gate baselines 39/39. Filed Bennett-mek9 (the
`lower_store!` AssertionError for a store through a loaded `jl_global` pointer).
- Julia's own front end removes simple scalar `Ref` and mutable-struct locals even at
  `optimize=false` (the IR is just `add`); only a `Ref` of a tuple keeps an alloca there.
- A conditionally stored local `Ref` gives a phi at `optimize=false` and a signed `icmp sgt` at
  `optimize=true`, which a different mrhg rule refuses — not usable as a "promoted memory" fixture.
  Selecting between two `Ref`s leaves GC allocations (`pgcstack`, `gc_alloc_obj`) in the IR.

**Bennett-sl4h: the 2026-10-03 decision could not close the bead, so it was replaced.** The triage
