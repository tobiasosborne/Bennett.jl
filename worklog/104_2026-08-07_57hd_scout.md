### Things a future agent would wish it knew

1. **THE CLUSTER IS NOT ALONE — three of them, same shape, in one body.**
   `w14_delta.jl` enumerated every `sub(ptrtoint,ptrtoint)` in the root: `%top`
   (wall 10), `%L21`, `%L43`. All are
   `array.ref.ptr_or_offset − array.ref.mem.data`. Any fix that clears only
   `%top` buys **two** walls and then re-walls on its own sibling. The bead
   (and every marker comment) describes the cluster in isolation; that framing
   is what makes route α look better than it is.

2. **The escape is far worse than "two closure-env stores".** `w07_gecomp.jl`
   walked the env+8 / env+16 use closures inside `_growend!` (74 and 47
   instructions). The index reaches `jl_alloc_genericmemory_unchecked`'s
   **allocation size** and `llvm.memmove`'s **length**. Under BVM's arena (no
   region table, three monotone cursors — `bennettvm-pdqx`) that is an
   *undetectable* clobber. **Confinement is not on the table**, and any
   proposal that offers a "third confinement" has not read the sink set. The
   bead's "either prove oracle-equality or confine it some other way"
   disjunction has an **empty** second arm.

3. **`_memdata_root(%7)` is `nothing`, not "a second root".** The bead says
   "two distinct `:load` roots". Measured: `%7` is a `{ptr,ptr}` **field-0**
   load, a shape `_memdata_root` does not recognise at all (it demands a
   literal `{i64,ptr}` at `[0,1]`). More importantly the two operands **do**
   share a syntactic base — both are read out of the same `gc_alloc_obj`ed
   `Array` header — which is what both surviving routes exploit. Do not repeat
   the "disjoint roots" framing; it is foz5's situation, not this one.

4. **Route α (value identity) was PROTOTYPED AND PROVEN, then found narrow.**
   A ~110-line `_pval` canonicaliser (aggregate-store field forwarding through
   `insertvalue` + same-slot reload + no-clobber scan) reduces both operands to
   the *same* `%memory_data` load, so `sub ≡ 0` in both worlds — 583s's own
   proof at displacement 0, no byte-tier premise needed. It closes `%top` and
   **not** `%L21`/`%L43`: the `_growend!` call in between is an unknown-effect
   barrier. Intraprocedural analysis stops at the first call that touches the
   object.

5. **α's intrinsic-write-footprint premise is LOAD-BEARING, and it is
   measured.** `w11_nopremise.jl` reclassifies `julia.gc_alloc_obj` as
   unknown-effect; the lemma collapses immediately
   (`BLOCKED reload … unknown-effect: … @julia.gc_alloc_obj`). Flip a premise
   and re-run — that is how an A-ledger entry earns its place rather than being
   asserted.

6. **α's witness is LIVE, unlike foz5's.** foz5 §4.2 refused to build on a
   **dead** `insertvalue` surviving only because of `optimize=false` (a Rule 5
   violation). α's `insertvalue` chain is consumed by the aggregate store. The
   objection does not transfer — worth stating explicitly, because "insertvalue
   witness" reads like the same hazard and is not.

7. **Route δ′ gives a clean three-way partition of the corpus.** A
   provenance-pair predicate (field-0 load of a literal `{ptr,ptr}` `P` vs the
   `.data` load reached through `P`'s field 1) matches **3/3** root clusters,
   **0/2** of the 583s-proved `_growend!` clusters, and **0/1** of foz5's
   `%idxend41` split-ref cluster. Three contracts, three disjoint provenance
   shapes: 583s = same `.data` load; δ′ = two halves of one in-body MemoryRef;
   foz5 §4a = two halves of a *split captured* ref. The no-steal is structural,
   the way foz5 arranged its own.

8. **`_root_scale` on a `gc_alloc_obj` root returns scale `1`, not `8`.**
   Measured: `(1, 24, "a julia.gc_alloc_obj BYTE-cell reservation of 24
   cell(s))"`. "Byte tier" means **one byte per cell**. A δ′ tier gate is
   `_root_scale(P)[1] == 1`. Easy to get backwards when reading bvmd's prose.

9. **δ′ amplifies `bennettvm-jb6w`.** It would recognise an unnamed literal
   `{ptr,ptr}` as "a MemoryRef". A C `struct { void*; void*; }` is far more
   common than the `{i64,ptr}` shape jb6w already flagged. Mitigated by the
   nested-pair conjunction and the byte-tier gate — but a proposer who does not
   confront it should be sent back.

10. **THE COMPOSITION RULE — the reason this is policy-level.** foz5's theorem
    is stated *relative to* "everything outside `τ` is computed by the
    pre-existing, already-sound model" (`instructions.jl:1586-1588`). Admitting
    an unproved **live** value puts an unsound producer outside `τ` and
    **retroactively voids ADR 0017 §4a**. Via `arena_top` it also breaks jbko's
    trajectory correspondence (injectivity survives a wrong allocation size;
    agreement with native does not). So the third contract must be
    oracle-match or halt-guaranteed — or the ADR must write the downgrade of
    §4a *and* jbko explicitly. This is the single hardest constraint and it is
    invisible from the bead.

11. **The escaping value does NOT reach foz5's §4a `sub`.** `%idxend41`'s offset

    coupling is the global one in (10). It does reach the **offset operand** of
    583s's `%L46`/`%L58` clusters; 583s's base-cancellation is base-independent
    and survives, but 583s is a *relative*-correctness proof and inherits
