## 1. Root cause

Multi-origin store dispatch uses the pointer’s origin predicate without conjoining the current block predicate. Independently reproduced: **64/256 Int8 inputs wrong**, folding on/off, despite passing reversibility.

## 3. Design

[Proposal B](/home/tobiasosborne/Projects/Bennett.jl/docs/design/2026-09-26-astra-fixes/37w3-proposal-B.md) composes `block_pred ∧ origin_pred` at each store site across shadow, checkpoint, and MUX-EXCH dispatch. It preserves reusable provenance, validates entry fast paths, leaves read-only loads unchanged, and retains persistent multi-origin rejection.
